# Challenge 13: "Pizza Order"

A tasty-looking device hides its credentials behind power signatures — bring a scope:

The device asks for a password inorder to login — it leaks the password through its power consumption.

This is a Simple Power Analysis (SPA) challenge: a basic oscilloscope is sufficient, but you must choose the right probe point and capture parameters.

The password can be exfiltrated by observing and interpreting power waveforms during the authentication routine (no invasive decapping or destructive faults required).

Your mission is clear:

1. Find a safe power-probing point (VCC or a convenient decoupling capacitor) and connect your oscilloscope ground/probe without shorting anything.
2. Trigger the device's authentication flow while recording power traces. 
3. Align and compare multiple traces, looking for repeatable waveform features that correspond to code paths or bit-wise comparisons.
4. Interpret the SPA patterns (peaks, pulses, durations) to reconstruct the password bytes.
Use the recovered password to authenticate and retrieve the flag from the device.
