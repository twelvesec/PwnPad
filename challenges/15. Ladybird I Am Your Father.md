# Challenge 15: "Ladybird I Am Your Father"

A compact safe hides its firmware — pull the image with JTAG and carefully patch it to open the vault.

Your mission is clear:

1. Locate and safely connect to the JTAG header.
2. Identify voltage domain (do not assume 3.3 V). Connect probe ground and VTREF properly. Use a suitable JTAG probe (SEGGER J-Link, ST-Link with JTAG support, or similar).
3. Use a JTAG/OpenOCD session to halt the core and read flash to a binary (dump_image/read_flash or equivalent). 
4. Perform static and dynamic analysis offline.
5. Apply the patch
